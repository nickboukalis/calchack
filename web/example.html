The question

If f(x) = 2x² − 7x + 3, what is the sum of the solutions of f(x) = 0?

⚡ Desmos is the fastest way here It shows both zeros instantly — no factoring, no quadratic formula, no sign mistakes.
QuadraticsMedium⏱ ~35 sec

Step by step

1

Graph the function

Type the function exactly as written. Desmos draws the parabola immediately — you're looking for where it crosses the x-axis.

y=2x^{2}-7x+3A parabola opening upward appears
What you see: a blue U-shaped parabola dips below the x-axis, crossing it twice — once just right of 0 and once near x = 3.
2

Tap the zeros

Tap the curve, then tap the two grey points where it crosses the x-axis. Desmos labels them for you — those are your solutions.

(0.5,0)First zero, revealed by tapping the curve
(3,0)Second zero
What you see: two labelled points sitting exactly where the curve meets the axis.
3

Add them in Desmos

Type the sum on a new line. Desmos doubles as a scratchpad, so you never do arithmetic in your head under time pressure.

0.5+3= 3.5 — done
What you see: the row shows 0.5+3 with = 3.5 on the right. That's your answer.
Answer
3.5  (or 7/2)

The zeros are x = 0.5 and x = 3, so their sum is 3.5.

⚠ Common trap

Picking 1.5 — that's the product of the roots (c/a = 3/2), not the sum. The SAT offers both on purpose.

The algebra way, if you prefer

Vieta's formulas: sum of roots = −b/a = 7/2. Faster if you already remember it — about ten seconds. CalcHack tells you when that's true instead of pushing you to the calculator every time.
