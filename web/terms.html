What CalcHack is

CalcHack reads a photo of a math question and explains how to solve it using the Desmos graphing calculator. It is a study aid. It is not a tutor, not a grading service, and not a substitute for learning the material.

It will sometimes be wrong

CalcHack uses an AI model to read questions and produce walkthroughs. AI models make mistakes — misreading a number, choosing a clumsy method, or producing a wrong answer outright. Always check the work against your own understanding. We do not guarantee accuracy, and we are not responsible for a grade, a test score, or any other outcome that follows from using it.

Use it honestly

CalcHack is for practice and studying. Do not use it during an exam, a proctored test, or anywhere your school or College Board prohibits outside assistance. Doing so is cheating, it is on you, and it can carry consequences far worse than a missed question.
