Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

CalcHack is a study tool that reads a photo of an SAT math question and explains how to solve it in Desmos. This page describes exactly what it collects and who it passes data to. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

What we don't collect

  • Card numbers. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe on Stripe's own pages. Card details never pass through our systems, and we never see them.
  • Your name, school, age, or location. We don't ask and we don't store them.
  • Analytics or advertising trackers. There are none on this site. Nobody is paying us to profile you.
  • Your reports, tied to you. If you report a wrong answer we keep the question and your note for six months to fix it. Your email is attached only if you were signed in.
  • Your photos, after processing. An uploaded image is used to answer that one question and is not retained.

Who we share data with

CalcHack is a small independent project and relies on a handful of established services to run. Each one receives only what it needs:

  • Google (Gemini API) — receives the photo and question text to generate the walkthrough.
  • Supabase — stores your email and password if you create an account.
  • Stripe — handles payment, and holds your billing details under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare — runs the server that processes scans and enforces free-scan limits.
  • Netlify — hosts this website.
  • Desmos — renders the interactive graph inside the app.

We do not sell personal data, and we have no arrangement with anyone to share it for advertising.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and nothing else. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar, or any other Google service, and CalcHack's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who this is for

CalcHack is built for students taking the SAT in the United States, and that's who it's offered to. We don't target or advertise to people outside the US. If you use it from elsewhere, the data described above is processed on servers in the United States.

Students under 18

CalcHack is built for high school students, so many of the people using it are minors. We collect as little as possible for that reason. CalcHack is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you are under 18, please get a parent or guardian's permission before buying a membership.

Deleting your data

Your scan history is stored in your own browser — Settings → Delete all inside the app clears it instantly, and so does clearing your browser data. To delete your account entirely, use Settings → Delete my account: it erases your synced history, cancels any subscription so you're never billed again, and removes the account. If anything is left over, email us and we'll finish it within seven days.

Security

Traffic to and from CalcHack is encrypted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed by Supabase and are never visible to us. No system is perfect, and we'd rather say that plainly than promise otherwise.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
